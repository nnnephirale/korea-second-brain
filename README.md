# Korea 2026 — TikTok Second Brain

A self-contained archive of 69 Korean TikTok links.

## Plane mode

1. Open this site on your iPhone while online and tap **Prepare offline**.
2. In Safari, use Share → **Add to Home Screen** for app-like access.
3. Download TikTok MP4s to the iPhone with Pull Mobile, then use **Add video files** and select them from Files. The saved videos stay in this phone’s IndexedDB; they are not uploaded to GitHub.

The archive still works offline with its saved notes and image previews even before any videos are added.
